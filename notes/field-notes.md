# Field notes

One entry per attempt at a lesson, written straight after doing it and before
fixing anything. The confusion is the data. Rewriting while you are still inside
the lesson destroys the evidence.

Record the boring entries too. "Took 12 minutes, not 25" is as useful as a
complaint.

## Template

```
### Lesson NN, attempt N, YYYY-MM-DD
Stated duration:      25 min
Actual:               ?? min
Where I got stuck:
What I wanted to try that the lesson did not offer:
What sounded worse than promised:
Verdict:              ship as is | rewrite section X | rethink
```
